Hipo! Uploader

Hipo! Uploader is a private, single-user tool used by the creator of the YouTube channel Hipo! (@Hipoteti_Camente). It automatically generates short animated "What would happen if...?" explainer videos (in Spanish) about geography, science and world events, and uploads the finished videos to that same channel using the YouTube API Services (videos.insert).

What it does

  • Generates one original educational short video per day.
  • Uploads it to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • It does not read, collect, store or share data of other YouTube users.
  • It is not offered to third parties and has no other users.

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
